Flock Rush

Privacy Policy · Effective October 15, 2026

Summary

Flock Rush is a casual sheepdog herding game for iPhone. We do not run our own servers, we do not require an account, and we do not sell your personal data. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The game is supported by ads from Google AdMob, which may collect certain data as described below, and sends anonymous usage data to PostHog so we can improve the game. You can turn usage data off in the app.

Who we are

Flock Rush is published by Victor Dovgaliuc ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at victordov@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your iPhone: coins, unlocked dog skins, the selected skin, match statistics, settings (sound, haptics), and whether you have seen the tutorial. Your progress itself stays on your device; the anonymous usage data described below is the only information the app sends to us. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset Progress in the app, or by deleting the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We show ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is accessed only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, Google uses non-tracking identifiers and shows non-personalized or less-personalized ads.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Usage and advertising data, such as ads shown and interactions with them.
  • Diagnostics, such as performance data used to keep ads working.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices in the app, and you can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded videos stay optional and are only shown when you choose to watch one.

Usage analytics (PostHog)

To learn which parts of the game players enjoy and to fix problems, the app sends anonymous usage data to PostHog, an analytics service, on servers in the United States:

  • Gameplay events, such as starting and finishing a match, the country played, the result, coins earned and dogs bought.
  • Purchase events: which in-app product was bought, its price and currency. We never receive your payment details.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and errors, the app version, device model and iOS version.
  • Approximate location (country and region) derived by PostHog from your IP address.

Each installation gets a random identifier. We do not send your name, email, Game Center alias or advertising identifier, we do not link usage data to your identity, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking. You can turn it off at any time with Settings → Share Anonymous Usage Data in the app. See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

Game Center (Apple)

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center data (such as your player alias, scores and achievements). We do not store this data. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app verifies purchases on your device using Apple's StoreKit.

Flock Rush Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is managed by RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives the App Store transactions for Pro and a random app user ID, so it can tell the app whether Pro is active, restore it and show your plan. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or create accounts.
  • We do not offer chat or let players post content.
  • We do not use usage data for advertising or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Computer-controlled players

Some opponents are computer-controlled. They are always marked with a robot badge. They do not collect any data.

Children

Flock Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Most requests are handled through your device settings and the choices described above. Usage data is anonymous; if you want it deleted, contact us and we will help. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy tools. You can always contact us for help.

Data retention and security

Progress data stays on your device until you reset it or delete the app. Usage data is kept in PostHog only as long as it helps us improve the game. Data collected by Google or Apple is retained according to their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
