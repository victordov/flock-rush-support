Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
  1. Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your sheepdog.
  2. When you leave your pasture, a fence trails behind you.
  3. Sheep run away from dogs, so push them into a group, then loop around them.
  4. Return to your pasture to close the loop. The land and every sheep inside become yours.
  5. While your fence is open, any rival who touches it knocks you out and your flock scatters. Run into a rival's open fence to knock them out!
  6. Every meadow is shaped like a real country. Herd all of it to win, then take a 15-second Victory Lap to collect bonus coins. If you're knocked out you can continue once. Golden sheep are worth extra.
Are the other players real?

No. Every match is you against seven computer players. They are always marked with a robot badge on their name tag and on the scoreboard. Their skill adapts to how you're doing.

Do I need an internet connection?

No, matches work offline. Leaderboards, achievements, purchases and ads need a connection.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, or open the Shop tab and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and Herding Pass premium tracks are restored to any device using the same Apple Account. Coin packs are consumable and cannot be restored.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every purchase in Flock Rush is a one-time purchase, and nothing renews.

I bought Remove Ads. Why can I still watch videos?

Remove Ads turns off all interstitial ads. Rewarded videos (for example, "double coins") stay available as an option and only play when you choose to watch them.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Open Settings → Report an Ad. Tell us what the ad showed and roughly when you saw it, and we will block it with our ad provider. You can also email us directly.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy Choices in the app (shown in regions where a consent choice is required), or change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings → Reset Progress. This erases the coins, dogs and statistics stored on this device. Purchases can be restored afterwards with Restore Purchases.
